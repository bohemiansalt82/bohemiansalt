Information

Bluepoint Condos offer you the rare opportunity to choose your view with a variety of floor plans including one to three bedroom layout choices. Unit sizes range from 123 to 277 sqm. The condos have private downstairs gardens with water features and dining area. Rooftop gardens offer sunshade, sala, dining area and a cocktail bar perfect for sunset drinks whilst taking in the wonderful views over the sea and Patong Bay. he low-rise development consists of three buildings each with three levels, a spacious shared swimming pool, car parking and beautiful landscaping are also included for your enjoyment. Bluepoint Condos are also fitted out with ‘Lutron Lighting Controls’. This replaces traditional wiring and offers the most advanced state of the art technologies for owner’s enjoyment. Bluepoint has fully embraced smart technology and each condo comes with a lighting control system that will enhance the ambience by providing control over audio-visual, in-room entertainment systems, blinds and shades. Mobile devices, such as iPhone, iPads or an iPod can control all the above. The major advantage is the ability to control any light or group of lights to create ‘light scenes’. Other benefits include reduced energy consumption and power costs through more efficient use, longer bulb life from dimming and reduced carbon emissions. In the creation of Bluepoint Condos, great respect has been given to the environment. Landscaping has been designed for the green rooftop terraces to reduce cooling costs and to help the development fit into its natural surroundings. The rooftop terraces also serve as a rainwater harvesting catchment area. Building materials and all engineering systems have been chosen to reduce operation and maintenance costs, and minimize negative effects on the surrounding ecosystem. Investors will have peace of mind that every effort has been taken to reduce their overall carbon footprint. With systems in place to harvest rainfall and reuse water, domestic appliances all have eco ratings of 5 out of 5, fitted sanitary ware items minimize water consumption, and high efficiency smart air conditioning units are placed in a well ventilated and shaded area.
